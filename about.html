Hello, I’mIbrahim

I’m Ibrahim Mosnad — a UX designer and the founder of Designwave, an independent studio for web design, digital products, brand identities and motion.

I help founders, startups and established teams turn complex ideas into experiences that feel simple, look distinctive and move with purpose. My work sits where strategy, interface and brand meet.

Right now my focus is web design: fast, memorable websites with thoughtful interaction and motion. But I still love the whole spectrum — from a logo sketched on paper to a design system used by a hundred people.

(01) Belief

Good design is a wave: it builds quietly, carries people forward and leaves a mark long after it has passed. That is the feeling I want every Designwave project to have.

(02) Principles

How I work

Clarity first

If people have to think about how to use it, it isn’t finished. Every decision starts with the user.

Details matter

Spacing, timing, type — the small things are what make people trust a product and remember a brand.

Motion with meaning

Animation should guide, explain and delight. Never decoration for its own sake.

Built to last

Systems over one-offs: designs that scale with your team and stay consistent as you grow.
