(04) Process

How we get there

01

Discover

We start with questions. Workshops, stakeholder interviews, audits and research to understand your business, your audience and what success looks like.

  • Workshops
  • Research
  • Audits
02

Define

Insights become direction: positioning, information architecture, user flows and wireframes that everyone can agree on before a single pixel is polished.

  • Strategy
  • IA
  • Wireframes
03

Design

Concepts, visual design, prototypes and motion. We iterate together in short cycles, testing ideas with real people wherever we can.

  • UI
  • Identity
  • Prototypes
  • Motion

Deliver

  • Launch
  • Support
