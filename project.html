Case study 2026

Aurora

A calmer way to manage money — marketing website and product UX for a modern fintech platform.

Client
Aurora Finance
Role
Lead UX & Web Designer
Services
Web Design, UX/UI Design, Design System
Timeline
12 weeks

(01) Challenge

Where we started

Aurora had a powerful product hidden behind a confusing sign-up flow and a website that looked like every other bank. They needed to feel trustworthy, human and simple — without losing the depth that power users rely on.

(02) Solution

What we made

We rebuilt the journey from first visit to first transfer. A new information architecture, a modular design system and a website with calm, confident motion now carry the brand from landing page to dashboard.

(03) Results

The impact

+48%Sign-up conversion
−35%Onboarding drop-off
4.8★Average app rating
