Design,end to end

From the first wireframe to the final animated logo, Designwave covers every part of design — with a special focus on websites that work as hard as they look.

01

Web Design

Current focus

Custom websites that are fast, intuitive and full of character — from strategy and wireframes to high-fidelity design, interaction and a smooth hand-off to development or a no-code build in Webflow or Framer.

  • Marketing websites
  • Landing pages
  • Portfolio & editorial sites
  • E-commerce design
  • Webflow / Framer builds
  • Interaction & micro-animation

UX / UI Design

  • App & dashboard UI
  • Usability testing
  • Design systems

(02) Process

How we get there

01

Discover

We start with questions. Workshops, stakeholder interviews, audits and research to understand your business, your audience and what success looks like.

  • Workshops
  • Research
